How Online Pujari works

Online Pujari is a pandit booking service for families across India. You choose the puja, the date and the language; we match a verified pandit, confirm the muhurat for your city on WhatsApp and take payment only after confirmation. The pandit arrives with a puja plan, and with the complete samagri kit if you chose that option. For families who cannot be present or cannot find a pandit locally, an online puja is available.

Pujas and sanskar we arrange

We arrange Griha Pravesh Puja, Satyanarayan Puja, Rudrabhishek Puja, Bhoomi Puja, Vastu Shanti Puja, Kaal Sarp Dosh Puja, Ganesh Puja, Lakshmi Puja, New Vehicle Puja, Mundan Sanskar and more. Each page explains the purpose, the samagri, the typical duration and the starting price, so you can decide with full information. Prices start from ₹1,100 and go up to ₹7,100 or more for larger ceremonies.

Languages and traditions

We match pandits by language and tradition: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati and Malayalam. Please share your family’s sampradaya and gotra so the sankalp and sequence are right for your household.

Choosing the muhurat

Muhurat is chosen from the tithi, nakshatra, weekday and your family’s Panchang tradition. Because local sunrise changes the timing from city to city, we confirm the muhurat for your own city. If you do not have a date, share a few days that suit you and we suggest the best options. Book at least a week ahead, and earlier for festival and wedding seasons.

Preparing for your puja

  • Clean the puja space and keep a low seat for the pandit.
  • Confirm the date, muhurat and arrival time on WhatsApp.
  • Arrange or confirm the samagri kit.
  • Keep names and gotra of family members ready.
  • Check society rules if a havan is planned.

Pricing and payment

Pujas start from ₹1,100. The price depends on the ritual, duration, samagri and the number of pandits. We confirm the total on WhatsApp before you pay, and dakshina for the pandit is separate and at your discretion. Refund and cancellation rules are in our refund policy.

Why families trust us

We speak to every pandit before listing them, confirm the plan with you before payment, and keep the process transparent: a named pandit, a confirmed muhurat and a clear price. We do not publish invented reviews or statistics. If something goes wrong, you can reach us directly on WhatsApp or through the contact page.

Respect for every tradition

India’s traditions differ widely: a Bengali Lakshmi puja, a Tamil Ganapathi homam and a Gujarati vastu pujan share a spirit but not a script. We never push a single template. Our pandits follow the order your family is used to, explain each step in your language and adapt the ritual to the space available, whether that is a small flat, a society hall or a shop. If your family has its own customs, tell us and we will pass them on to the pandit before the day.

Privacy and safety

We ask only for the details needed to arrange your puja: your name, phone number, city, date and puja type. We do not ask for ID numbers or bank passwords, and we never share your details for advertising. Payment is taken only after the pandit and muhurat are confirmed. Read our privacy policy and terms for the full details.

Tips for first-time families

If this is your first time arranging a puja, start by deciding the occasion and a range of dates, then ask us for the muhurat options. Speak to elders about family customs, and note the gotra and names of those taking part. Keep the puja space clean and simple, arrange fresh flowers and fruit on the morning of the puja and let the pandit guide the rest. The glossary and blog explain common words so that nothing feels unfamiliar on the day.
