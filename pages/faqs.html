Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

What pujas can I book?

Griha Pravesh, Satyanarayan, Rudrabhishek, Bhoomi, Vastu Shanti, Kaal Sarp Dosh, Lakshmi, Ganesh, office and shop opening, sanskar, homams and more. See the full list on our pujas page.

Will I get a pandit who speaks my language?

We match by language and city first. If your language is not available on your date, we tell you before you pay and suggest options, including an online puja.

Do I need to pay in advance?

No payment is taken until the pandit and muhurat are confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

When will I know which pandit is assigned?

After confirmation we share the pandit’s name, language and arrival time, plus a samagri list.

Is samagri included?

You choose: pandit only, or pandit with the full samagri kit. Each puja page shows both prices.

Can I do the puja online?

Yes. A pandit leads the puja over video and performs the sankalp in your name and gotra, which suits families abroad.

What if I do not know the muhurat?

Choose “help me find a date” in the form. We suggest auspicious dates from the Panchang for your city.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, free up to 48 hours before the puja.

Pandit ji near me

Looking for a pandit near you? We arrange pandits for homes, societies, offices and shops across Noida, Greater Noida, Greater Noida West, Ghaziabad, Delhi and Gurugram. See every area on our locations page.

Muhurat and guidance

Not sure which puja suits the occasion or when to hold it? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you pick the right puja and a muhurat for Griha Pravesh, Mundan, Namkaran, a business opening or any ceremony.
