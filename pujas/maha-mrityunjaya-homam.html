Maha Mrityunjaya Homam – book pandit online

Homam for health, protection and long life. Starting from ₹6,100, 3–4 hours.

What is Maha Mrityunjaya Homam?

Chanting the Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra with homam is done for recovery, protection and peace of mind.

Samagri (puja items)

  • Havan kund, mango wood
  • Ghee, black sesame
  • Havan samagri
  • Kalash, flowers
  • Shiva photo

Our pandit can bring the complete samagri kit, or share a list if you prefer to arrange it.

How we perform it

Duration is about 3–4 hours. The pandit performs the sankalp with your name and gotra, Ganesh pujan, the main ritual with mantras, aarti and closing prasad. The puja can be at home, at a venue or online by video.

Frequently asked questions

How many chants are done?

Common counts are 1,25,000 or 11,000; the pandit advises by budget and need.

Can I take part online?

Yes, with sankalp in your name.
