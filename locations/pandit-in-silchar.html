Pujas families in Silchar book most often

Here are popular choices, each with its purpose, usual duration and starting price. Every page lists what the ritual needs and what it costs.

  • Shradh and Pitru Puja: Shradh, including Pind Daan and Tarpan, is performed to honour ancestors, mostly during Pitru Paksha. Typically 2 hours, from ₹3,500.
  • Seemantham / Godh Bharai: Seemantham, also called Godh Bharai or Valaikaapu, blesses the expectant mother and child in the 7th month. Typically 2 hours, from ₹3,100.
  • Office Opening Puja: An office opening puja invokes Lord Ganesha and Goddess Lakshmi for smooth work, good clients and a positive workplace before the first day of business. Typically 1–1.5 hours, from ₹3,100.
  • Shop and Factory Opening Puja: A shop or factory opening puja (Dukan or Karkhana Muhurat) blesses the business, the tools and the first sale. Factories often add a machine puja. Typically 1–2 hours, from ₹3,500.
  • Sundarkand Path: Sundarkand Path is the recitation of the fifth book of the Ramcharitmanas about Hanuman’s journey to Lanka. It is read on Tuesdays and Saturdays for courage, peace and removal of obstacles. Typically 2–3 hours, from ₹2,100.
  • Griha Pravesh Puja: Griha Pravesh invites positive energy, Ganesh and Lakshmi’s blessings and removes negativity before you start living in a new home. Typically 2–3 hours, from ₹5,100.

Other options include Griha Pravesh Puja, Satyanarayan Puja, Rudrabhishek Puja, Office Opening Puja, Shop and Factory Opening Puja, Sundarkand Path and other sanskar. If your puja is not listed, choose “Other puja” in the booking form and describe it.

Venue planning: home, apartment, society or office

In Silchar a puja may be held at home, in an apartment complex, at a community hall, or at an office or shop.

  • Flats: check society rules on havan smoke, lift use for the samagri and visitor entry.
  • Independent homes: clear a clean, level space facing the direction your pandit suggests, usually east or north.
  • Business premises: early morning or after closing usually works best.
  • Access: share the floor, landmark and parking details so the pandit arrives on time with the kit.

Checklist before the pandit arrives in Silchar

  1. Re-check the muhurat and timing a day before.
  2. Tidy the space and arrange a mat or low seat.
  3. Arrange the samagri and buy fresh flowers and fruit.
  4. Note each participant’s name and gotra.
  5. Inform neighbours or society staff if there will be a havan.
  6. Arrange prasad and drinking water.

Samagri: bring your own or let the pandit arrange it

Samagri is the set of items a puja needs, from flowers and fruit to ghee, camphor and havan material. Families short on time usually take the kit and buy only fresh flowers and fruit. Buy flowers, banana leaves and fruit locally in Silchar on the day for freshness.
