Pujas families in Dehradun book most often

These are the pujas most commonly requested. Open any page to see samagri, timing and what is included.

  • Maha Mrityunjaya Homam: Chanting the Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra with homam is done for recovery, protection and peace of mind. Typically 3–4 hours, from ₹6,100.
  • Shradh and Pitru Puja: Shradh, including Pind Daan and Tarpan, is performed to honour ancestors, mostly during Pitru Paksha. Typically 2 hours, from ₹3,500.
  • Seemantham / Godh Bharai: Seemantham, also called Godh Bharai or Valaikaapu, blesses the expectant mother and child in the 7th month. Typically 2 hours, from ₹3,100.
  • Office Opening Puja: An office opening puja invokes Lord Ganesha and Goddess Lakshmi for smooth work, good clients and a positive workplace before the first day of business. Typically 1–1.5 hours, from ₹3,100.
  • Shop and Factory Opening Puja: A shop or factory opening puja (Dukan or Karkhana Muhurat) blesses the business, the tools and the first sale. Factories often add a machine puja. Typically 1–2 hours, from ₹3,500.
  • Sundarkand Path: Sundarkand Path is the recitation of the fifth book of the Ramcharitmanas about Hanuman’s journey to Lanka. It is read on Tuesdays and Saturdays for courage, peace and removal of obstacles. Typically 2–3 hours, from ₹2,100.

Other options include Griha Pravesh Puja, Satyanarayan Puja, Rudrabhishek Puja, Office Opening Puja, Shop and Factory Opening Puja, Sundarkand Path and other sanskar. If your puja is not listed, choose “Other puja” in the booking form and describe it.

Checklist before the pandit arrives in Dehradun

  1. Re-check the muhurat and timing a day before.
  2. Tidy the space and arrange a mat or low seat.
  3. Collect samagri or confirm the kit; keep fresh flowers and fruit.
  4. Note each participant’s name and gotra.
  5. Tell society staff if a havan is planned.
  6. Keep prasad, water and a place for the pandit to rest.
