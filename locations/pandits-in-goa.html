Booking a pandit in Goa

Hindu families in Goa often follow Konkani and Marathi customs, so we match the pandit to your tradition. Online Pujari brings that variety into one place: you choose your city in Goa, the puja and the language, and we confirm a pandit and muhurat on WhatsApp before you pay. We currently list pandit booking for 2 cities in Goa: Panaji, Margao. If your town is not listed, message us anyway; we often arrange pandits in nearby towns or an online puja.

Languages and traditions in Goa

We offer pandits who perform puja in Marathi, Hindi, Kannada, and Konkani-speaking pandits can be arranged on request. Language matters for more than conversation: the mantras, the explanation of each step and the family customs all feel more meaningful when the pandit speaks the way your household does. Please mention your sampradaya or family tradition when you book so the pandit prepares the correct sankalp and sequence.

Festivals and occasions in Goa

The busiest times for pandits in Goa are around Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali and Shigmo. During these weeks many families book a satyanarayan katha, a rudrabhishek, a lakshmi puja or a sundarkand path, and slots fill early. If your date is close to a festival, book at least two weeks ahead. Our festival calendar and blog explain dates and rules.

Popular pujas families book in Goa

  • Griha Pravesh Puja: Housewarming ritual to bless your new home. Typically 2–3 hours, from ₹5,100.
  • Satyanarayan Puja: Katha for prosperity, peace and gratitude. Typically 2 hours, from ₹3,100.
  • Rudrabhishek Puja: Abhishek of Lord Shiva to remove obstacles and planetary doshas. Typically 1.5–2 hours, from ₹4,100.
  • Bhoomi Puja: Ground-breaking ceremony before construction begins. Typically 2 hours, from ₹4,100.
  • Vastu Shanti Puja: Balance the energies of your home or office. Typically 3 hours, from ₹5,100.
  • Kaal Sarp Dosh Puja: Remedy ritual for Kaal Sarp Dosh in the birth chart. Typically 3–4 hours, from ₹7,100.
  • Ganesh Puja: Begin anything new with Lord Ganesha’s blessing. Typically 1–1.5 hours, from ₹2,100.
  • Lakshmi Puja: Diwali, Dhanteras and Varalakshmi Vratam puja. Typically 1.5–2 hours, from ₹3,100.

How the muhurat is chosen

Auspicious timings depend on the tithi, nakshatra, weekday and the local sunrise, which shifts across Goa. Instead of copying one calendar for the whole state, we confirm the muhurat for your own city and your family’s Panchang tradition. If you do not have a date, share a few days that suit you and we will suggest the best options.

Home, apartment and office pujas in Goa

Pujas happen in flats, independent houses, society halls, temples, offices and shops. For apartments check whether a havan is allowed, how the samagri can be carried in and whether guests need gate passes. For shops and offices pick a time outside peak hours. Share your address, floor and parking details so the pandit arrives on time.

Samagri and the cost of a puja

Pujas start from ₹1,100 and larger ceremonies go up to ₹7,100 or more depending on the ritual, duration and number of pandits. Each puja page shows prices with and without the samagri kit. The total is confirmed before you pay; dakshina and guest hospitality are separate and up to you.

Online puja from anywhere

If you live outside Goa, or a pandit in your language is not free on your date, a pandit can lead the puja by video and perform the sankalp in your name and gotra. This is popular for festival pujas, birthday homams and shraddh when families cannot travel home.
