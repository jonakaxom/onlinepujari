Pradosh Vrat: Date, Puja Vidhi and Benefits

Pradosh vrat dates (including February 2026), puja vidhi for Shiva and benefits. Observed on Trayodashi.

By Online Pujari pandit team · Updated 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Pradosh Vrat is observed on Trayodashi of both fortnights in the twilight period to please Lord Shiva.

In this guide
  1. Vidhi
  2. Dates
  3. Rudrabhishek

Vidhi

  • Fast through the day.
  • Do Shiva puja in the evening Pradosh kaal.
  • Offer milk, bel leaves and water on the Shivling.

Dates

Pradosh falls twice a month. Dates can differ by city and Panchang tradition, so confirm the exact muhurat with your pandit.

Rudrabhishek

Many devotees do Rudrabhishek on Pradosh.
