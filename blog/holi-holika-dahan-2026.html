Holika Dahan and Holi 2026: Muhurat, Puja Vidhi and Story

Holika Dahan muhurat 2026, Holi puja 2026, why we light the Holika bonfire, and the Prahlad story.

By Online Pujari pandit team · Updated 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Holika Dahan is lit on Phalguna Purnima evening, and Holi (rang) follows next day.

In this guide
  1. Dates
  2. Why the bonfire

Dates

2026: Holika Dahan on 3 March, Holi on 4 March. Dates can differ by city and Panchang tradition, so confirm the exact muhurat with your pandit.

Why the bonfire

It remembers the victory of devotee Prahlad over Holika. Wood, cow-dung cakes and coconut are offered; families circle the fire three or seven times.

Planning with the Panchang

The Panchang is the Hindu almanac that lists the tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana for each day. Local sunrise and moonrise change the effective timings from city to city, and regional calendars differ between solar and lunar traditions. That is why a festival can fall on different dates in different states and why we always confirm the date for your city.

Why local practice differs

Hindu practice is not one uniform rulebook. Rituals, mantras, offerings and even the date of a festival vary between regions, languages and family lineages. A tradition followed in Assam may differ from one in Tamil Nadu, and both are valid for the families who follow them. When you read any guide, including this one, treat it as a general explanation and give priority to what your own elders and family priest practise.

Planning ahead

Good planning removes most stress from a puja. Fix a range of dates, ask for the muhurat, confirm the pandit, arrange samagri a day before and keep names and gotra ready. If guests are travelling, share the muhurat with them early. For festivals, book earlier than usual because pandits are in high demand in those weeks.
