Navratri 2026 Dates, Dussehra 2025 and 2026 & Durga Puja Guide

Navratri 2026 dates (Chaitra and Sharad), last Navratri, Dussehra (Vijayadashami) 2025 and 2026 dates, ghatasthapana and Durga puja.

By Online Pujari pandit team · Updated 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Navratri is nine nights of Goddess Durga, observed twice a year, and ends with Dussehra.

In this guide
  1. Dates
  2. What is done each day
  3. Homams

Dates

  • Chaitra Navratri 2026: begins 19 March.
  • Sharad Navratri 2026: begins 11 October; Dussehra on 20 October.
  • Dussehra 2025: 2 October.

Dates can differ by city and Panchang tradition, so confirm the exact muhurat with your pandit.

What is done each day

Ghatasthapana on day 1, then nine forms of Durga (Shailputri to Siddhidatri) are worshipped with fasting, Durga Saptashati or Durga Kavach paath, and Kanya Pujan on Ashtami or Navami.

Homams

Durga Homam and Chandi Homam are performed during Navratri for protection and prosperity. Ask us for pandits and quotes.
