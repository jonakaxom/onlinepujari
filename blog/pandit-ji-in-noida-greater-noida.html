Pujas families in Noida and Greater Noida book most often

Here are popular choices, each with its purpose, usual duration and starting price. Every page lists what the ritual needs and what it costs.

  • Satyanarayan Puja: Performed on full-moon days, after a housewarming, wedding or any milestone to thank Lord Vishnu and seek family wellbeing. Typically 2 hours, from ₹3,100.
  • Rudrabhishek Puja: Rudrabhishek with milk, honey, curd and Ganga jal is performed for health, peace, relief from doshas and success. Typically 1.5–2 hours, from ₹4,100.
  • Bhoomi Puja: Bhoomi Puja seeks the blessings of Mother Earth, Vastu Purush and the directional deities before the first foundation is dug. Typically 2 hours, from ₹4,100.
  • Vastu Shanti Puja: Vastu Shanti corrects directional imbalances and brings harmony to a home, shop or office, especially after renovation or purchase. Typically 3 hours, from ₹5,100.
  • Kaal Sarp Dosh Puja: This puja is performed to reduce delays, obstacles and anxiety attributed to Rahu–Ketu positions in a kundli. Typically 3–4 hours, from ₹7,100.
  • Ganesh Puja: Ganesh Puja removes obstacles before weddings, business openings, new vehicles and festivals such as Ganesh Chaturthi. Typically 1–1.5 hours, from ₹2,100.

Choosing the right muhurat in Noida and Greater Noida

A muhurat is the auspicious window chosen from tithi, nakshatra, weekday and your family’s Panchang tradition. Local sunrise shifts the timings from one city to another, so we confirm the muhurat for Noida and Greater Noida specifically.

If you have no date yet, send a few days that work and we will recommend the most suitable muhurat. Weekends and festival weeks fill quickly, so ask at least a week ahead.

Samagri: bring your own or let the pandit arrange it

Samagri means the materials used in the puja: flowers, fruits, ghee, camphor, a kalash and havan items. You may gather the items yourself or choose a complete kit brought by the pandit. Perishables such as flowers and mango leaves are best bought on the morning of the puja in Noida and Greater Noida.

Venue planning: home, apartment, society or office

The venue shapes the planning more than people expect.

  • Flats: check society rules on havan smoke, lift use for the samagri and visitor entry.
  • Houses: keep a tidy, level corner ready, commonly facing east or north.
  • Business premises: early morning or after closing usually works best.
  • Access: share the floor, landmark and parking details so the pandit arrives on time with the kit.
